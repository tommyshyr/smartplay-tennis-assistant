規則與資料來源

本工具使用康文署的公開空位資料安排候選時段並提供本機通知。請在官方 My SmartPLAY App 或網站登入、選場及付款。

香港放位時間

  • 07:00　透過「智方便」登入 My SmartPLAY App，可優先預訂。未滿 11 歲的用戶仍可於 07:00 使用 App 預訂。
  • 07:15　可使用一般 App 登入、官方網頁、自助服務站或服務櫃枱預訂。

七天預訂期包括預訂當天。康文署使用條件禁止以程式或自動化工具進行預訂、交易或干擾系統，違規帳戶可被暫停 360 日。

空位數據的範圍

官方公開 JSON 標示每五分鐘更新，包含場地、設施類型、日期、時段和可租訂球場數量。空位在更新之間仍可能變動。相鄰時段各有空位時，請到官方 App 核對是否為同一球場。

空位通知使用程式成功讀取的官方資料。手動匯入的快照可用於排場。公開資料未列出場地或時段時，可到官方 App 核對最新供應。

Rules and data sources

SmartPLAY Tennis Assistant uses LCSD's public availability feed to rank possible sessions and send local alerts. Sign in, select a court, and pay in the official My SmartPLAY app or website.

Daily release times in Hong Kong

  • 07:00 Priority booking in My SmartPLAY using iAM Smart. Eligible users under 11 can book through the app under LCSD's separate arrangement.
  • 07:15 Booking opens through regular app login, the official website, self-service stations, and service counters.

The seven-day booking window includes the day you book. LCSD prohibits using software to automate bookings or interfere with SmartPLAY. An account that breaches these terms can be suspended for 360 days.

LCSD release notice · SmartPLAY Conditions of Use

Availability data

LCSD refreshes the public feed about every five minutes. It lists the venue, facility type, date, time slot, and available court count. For two consecutive hours, verify the same court number in both slots. The official app has the current availability and fees.

Alerts use successful live reads of the public feed. The planner can also use a manually imported snapshot. Check the official app if the feed omits a venue or time.
